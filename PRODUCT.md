# Product

## Register

product

## Users

JobScout is a local, single-user workspace for an early-career U.S. job seeker who needs future visa sponsorship. The user works in focused sessions: discover fresh roles, eliminate hard eligibility conflicts, inspect evidence, tailor an application, and track outcomes.

## Product Purpose

JobScout turns fragmented employer and ATS listings into a small, trustworthy queue of jobs worth applying to. Success means fewer wasted applications, faster decisions, fresher direct-source roles, truthful resume tailoring, and a clear record of what was applied to and what converted.

## Brand Personality

Focused, candid, quietly confident. The product should feel like a careful research assistant and application command center, not a noisy job board or a gamified growth dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized marketing-style headers inside the workspace, decorative SaaS cards, bright dashboard clutter, hidden critical filters, horizontal page scrolling, and layouts that sacrifice job-list or job-detail space for chrome. Tsenta is a useful functional reference, not a visual template to copy.

## Design Principles

1. Put fresh, apply-worthy jobs above configuration and explanation.
2. Keep eligibility evidence visible and explain every consequential ranking decision.
3. Spend model calls only after deterministic filters have removed obvious non-matches.
4. Preserve direct employer links, source provenance, and truthful candidate evidence.
5. Use progressive disclosure so advanced controls exist without dominating the workspace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation, labels, and error states. Do not rely on color alone for sponsorship, eligibility, or application status. Respect reduced motion and preserve complete functionality at narrow desktop and mobile widths.
